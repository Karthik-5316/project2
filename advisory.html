Field notes

Use these prompts during planning and routine crop observation.

What should I check before changing irrigation?

Look at crop stage, recent weather, soil moisture and how evenly water reaches the field.

Why keep field records?

Simple records help compare seasons, identify recurring issues and review what worked.

When should I seek local expert support?

When a crop shows unfamiliar stress, disease symptoms, major pest pressure or other conditions that need diagnosis.

How can I make crop information easier to use?

Focus on a few key indicators: crop stage, moisture, plant appearance, pest observations and upcoming weather.
