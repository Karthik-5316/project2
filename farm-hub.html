Field observation

Walk the field regularly. Small changes often provide early clues.

A practical planning checklist

  1. Review the previous crop and field history.
  2. Check soil condition and drainage.
  3. Match crop choice to season and local conditions.
  4. Plan water access before critical growth stages.
  5. Schedule regular scouting and record observations.
